Today, patient records sit in boxes

Prescriptions are bundled with rubber bands and stacked in storerooms. They are slow to find and easy to lose, even though records must be kept for 3 to 5 years.

97%
of patients lose their records within 6 months, including people managing HIV/AIDS, diabetes and hypertension.

How it works

  1. Enrol

    Patient is registered once at a clinic or pharmacy.

  2. Issue card

    A smart health card is linked to their record.

  3. Scan

    Staff tap the card and confirm with a fingerprint.

  4. Dispense

    Records update and medication is issued.
